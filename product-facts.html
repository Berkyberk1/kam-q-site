KAM-Q product facts

One page with the plain facts, for people and for anyone writing about KAM-Q. Last reviewed 24 November 2026.

What it is

KAM-Q is private email search software. It builds a search index of your mailbox and finds mail by sender, subject or attachment file name. It also has a People view that ranks everyone who emails you and lists every file each person has sent.

Publisher and contact

PRB Interactive Inc. Support: support@kam-q.com. Website: kam-q.com.

Platforms and versions

  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11 with classic Outlook. Version 2.0.3. The new Outlook is not supported.
  • Mac: macOS 11 or later. Separate apps for classic Outlook for Mac and for Apple Mail, version 3.4. One license covers both.
  • Browser: a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, with a Microsoft 365, Outlook.com or Gmail account.
  • Gmail add-on: coming soon.

Pricing and license

  • Free for 7 days, no card required.
  • Individual: $39.99 for the first year, then $19.99 a year. Launch offer: $24.99 for the first year with code FIRSTYEAR, until December 31, 2026.
  • One license covers four installations of the Windows and Mac apps on one person’s own computers. The browser version is free and needs no license.
  • Teams: $21 per person per year for 2 to 19 people, $18 per person for 20 to 59. Larger teams by quote.
  • 30 day refund for any reason.
  • If a subscription ends, KAM-Q keeps working and everything already indexed stays searchable. New mail is added only while the subscription is active.

Privacy

  • On Windows and Mac the index is kept on your own computer. In the browser it is kept in your browser.
  • No KAM-Q server receives your mail or your searches. The apps connect to the internet for license checks and update checks.
  • The Outlook versions are read only.
  • Details: privacy policy.

What it does not do

  • It does not read the text inside attached files. It finds attachments by file name and by the message they arrived with.
  • It does not search documents or files on your computer.
  • It has no server edition.
