Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

In short: KAM-Q does not send your email to us or to anyone else. Your mail is read on your own computer or in your own browser, and the search index stays there.

Who we are

KAM-Q is made by PRB Interactive Inc., a Delaware corporation ("we", "us"). Contact: support@kam-q.com.

What KAM-Q does

KAM-Q builds a search index of your mailbox so you can find messages, people, and attachments quickly. The index holds each message's sender, recipients, subject, date, folder, and attachment names.

In the Windows and Mac apps, the text of your messages is not indexed. The web app is slightly different, because of how Gmail returns mail. That is set out below.

Information we collect

We collect none of your mail or personal information on our servers. We do not run servers that receive your email, your index, or your sign-in details.

  • Windows and Mac apps. They read your mail through Outlook or Apple Mail on your own computer and keep their index in a folder on that computer. Your mail never leaves your computer. If you bought a license from our store, KAM-Q contacts the store from time to time to check that the license is still valid. That check sends your license key and nothing else: no mail, no names, no index. Licenses issued directly by us are checked on your computer and make no connection at all.
  • Web app. Your browser connects directly to Microsoft or Google. Sign-in tokens, settings, and the index are stored in your browser only. Signing out and erasing KAM-Q data removes them.
  • Gmail in the web app. KAM-Q reads message headers (sender, recipients, subject, date, labels), attachment file names, and a short preview of each message that Gmail provides, called a snippet. Snippets come from the message text. KAM-Q stores these in your browser so you can search them. It does not store full message bodies. When you open or save an attachment, KAM-Q asks Gmail for that message, and Gmail's response can include inline message content. KAM-Q uses it to produce the file you chose and does not keep it. Your browser connects directly to Google, and none of this goes to PRB Interactive Inc. or to any server we operate. If you turn on Cleanup, KAM-Q also asks for permission to move messages to Trash and to restore them. It does not permanently delete messages.
  • This website. It is a static site. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested. We also use Plausible Analytics to count visits to these pages. Plausible sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and collects no personal information. It records the page visited, the site that referred you, and a general country, device type, and browser. We use it only to see which pages people find useful. Plausible is not used in the KAM-Q web app, and it never receives anything about your mail.

Permissions KAM-Q asks for

  • Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com: User.Read to show who is signed in, and Mail.Read to read mail. Both are read-only.
  • Gmail: gmail.readonly to read your mail, asked for when you sign in. If you turn on the optional Cleanup feature, KAM-Q also asks for gmail.modify to move messages to Trash and to restore them. That one is only requested at the moment you choose to move mail. It never deletes messages permanently.

How we use information

Information from your mailbox is used only to provide search and People features to you, inside your own browser or on your own computer. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared.

KAM-Q's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your index lives on your computer or in your browser. Delete the KAM-Q data folder, or sign out and erase data in the web app, and it is gone. You can also remove KAM-Q's access at any time in your Google account or your Microsoft account.

How we protect your data

KAM-Q is built so that the strongest protection is structural: we hold none of your mail, so there is no copy of it for us to lose. The specific measures below apply to the data KAM-Q does handle, including sensitive data such as the contents of your mailbox and the tokens that give access to it.

  • Encryption in transit. Every connection KAM-Q makes uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or later. This covers the connections your browser makes to Google and to Microsoft, the licence check the desktop apps make to our store provider, and this website. KAM-Q makes no unencrypted network connections.
  • Encryption at rest. On Windows and Mac, the index is written inside your own user profile and is protected by the account permissions and the disk encryption of your computer, such as BitLocker or FileVault. In the web app, the index and your sign-in tokens are held in your browser's own storage for the KAM-Q site, which your browser isolates from every other site and protects with the operating system's user account controls.
  • Access control. Data stays inside your own user account on your own device. No PRB Interactive Inc. employee or contractor can reach your mailbox, your index, or your tokens, because none of it is transmitted to or stored on any system we operate. There is no administrative back door, because there is no server to put one in.
  • Authentication and tokens. Sign-in happens on Google's and Microsoft's own pages, never on ours. KAM-Q never sees, asks for, or stores your password. For Microsoft, KAM-Q uses the Microsoft Authentication Library and the OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow with PKCE. For Google, it uses Google Identity Services, which returns a short lived access token to your browser. In both cases the token is held only in your browser, KAM-Q holds no client secret, and the provider only returns a token to a web address we have registered with them in advance. Signing out revokes the Google token with Google and clears the Microsoft session.
  • Least privilege. KAM-Q requests the narrowest permissions that allow the product to work, and requests them incrementally. The optional Cleanup permission is asked for only at the moment you choose to move mail, not at sign-in. The permissions in use are listed in full above.
  • Retention and deletion. There is no retention period on our side, because we store nothing. On your side the index persists until you remove it. In the web app, Erase KAM-Q data deletes the index and the stored tokens immediately. On Windows and Mac, deleting the KAM-Q data folder removes the index. You can revoke KAM-Q's access at any time from your Google or Microsoft account, which invalidates its tokens without needing anything from us.
  • Code integrity. The Windows and Mac applications are code signed by PRB Interactive Inc., and the Mac applications are notarized by Apple, so you can confirm that what you installed is what we published and that it has not been altered.
  • Third parties. The only third parties involved are the identity and mail providers you choose to connect, our payment and licensing provider, which receives a licence key and your purchase details and never receives mail data, and the hosting and analytics providers for this website. No provider receives the contents of your mailbox.
  • Incident response. If we ever become aware of a security issue affecting KAM-Q, we will publish it on this site and notify affected customers by email without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of confirming it. To report a suspected vulnerability, write to support@kam-q.com and we will acknowledge it within two business days.

Because the data lives with you, the protections on your own devices matter. Keep your computer and browser up to date, use a password or passcode on your account, and turn on full disk encryption.
