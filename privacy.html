Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

In short: KAM-Q does not send your email to us or to anyone else. Your mail is read on your own computer or in your own browser, and the search index stays there.

Who we are

KAM-Q is made by PRB Interactive Inc., a Delaware corporation ("we", "us"). Contact: support@kam-q.com.

What KAM-Q does

KAM-Q builds a search index of your mailbox so you can find messages, people, and attachments quickly. The index holds each message's sender, recipients, subject, date, folder, and attachment names.

In the Windows and Mac apps, the text of your messages is not indexed. The web app is slightly different, because of how Gmail returns mail. That is set out below.

Information we collect

We collect none of your mail or personal information on our servers. We do not run servers that receive your email, your index, or your sign-in details.

  • Windows and Mac apps. They read your mail through Outlook or Apple Mail on your own computer and keep their index in a folder on that computer. Your mail never leaves your computer. If you bought a license from our store, KAM-Q contacts the store from time to time to check that the license is still valid. That check sends your license key and nothing else: no mail, no names, no index. Licenses issued directly by us are checked on your computer and make no connection at all.
  • Web app. Your browser connects directly to Microsoft or Google. Sign-in tokens, settings, and the index are stored in your browser only. Signing out and erasing KAM-Q data removes them.
  • Gmail in the web app. KAM-Q reads message headers (sender, recipients, subject, date, labels), attachment file names, and a short preview of each message that Gmail provides, called a snippet. Snippets come from the message text. KAM-Q stores these in your browser so you can search them. It does not store full message bodies. When you open or save an attachment, KAM-Q asks Gmail for that message, and Gmail's response can include inline message content. KAM-Q uses it to produce the file you chose and does not keep it. Your browser connects directly to Google, and none of this goes to PRB Interactive Inc. or to any server we operate. If you turn on Cleanup, KAM-Q also asks for permission to move messages to Trash and to restore them. It does not permanently delete messages.
  • This website. It is a static site. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested. We also use Plausible Analytics to count visits to these pages. Plausible sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and collects no personal information. It records the page visited, the site that referred you, and a general country, device type, and browser. We use it only to see which pages people find useful. Plausible is not used in the KAM-Q web app, and it never receives anything about your mail.

Permissions KAM-Q asks for

  • Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com: User.Read to show who is signed in, and Mail.Read to read mail. Both are read-only.
  • Gmail: gmail.readonly to read your mail, asked for when you sign in. If you turn on the optional Cleanup feature, KAM-Q also asks for gmail.modify to move messages to Trash and to restore them. That one is only requested at the moment you choose to move mail. It never deletes messages permanently.

How we use information

Information from your mailbox is used only to provide search and People features to you, inside your own browser or on your own computer. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared.

KAM-Q's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your index lives on your computer or in your browser. Delete the KAM-Q data folder, or sign out and erase data in the web app, and it is gone. You can also remove KAM-Q's access at any time in your Google account or your Microsoft account.

Security

Because nothing is sent to us, the main protection is that your mail stays where it already lives. Keep your computer and browser up to date and protected with a password.

Children

KAM-Q is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@kam-q.com.
