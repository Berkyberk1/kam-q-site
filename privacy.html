Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

In short: KAM-Q does not send your mailbox data or search index to PRB Interactive Inc. Its desktop apps process mail on your device; the web app connects your browser directly to Microsoft or Google.

Who we are

KAM-Q is made by PRB Interactive Inc., a Delaware corporation ("we", "us"). Contact: support@kam-q.com.

What KAM-Q does

KAM-Q builds a search index of your mailbox so you can find messages, people, and attachments quickly. The index holds each message's sender, recipients, subject, date, folder, and attachment names.

The desktop search indexes do not store message previews or full message bodies. The web app is different: it also keeps a short preview of each message, which comes from the message text. That is set out below.

Information we collect

We do not collect your mailbox data on our servers. We do not run servers that receive your email, your search index, or your sign-in details. We do receive some other information, described below: purchase and license details, anything you choose to send us, and standard website logs and visit counts.

  • Windows and Mac apps. They read your mail through Outlook or Apple Mail on your own computer and keep their index in a folder on that computer. Your mail never leaves your computer. When you open an attachment from KAM-Q, a copy is saved on your computer so it can open: in a KAM-Q folder inside the Windows temporary folder, or in your Downloads folder on a Mac. Those copies stay there until you or the system delete them, and deleting the index does not delete them. If you bought a license from our store, KAM-Q contacts the store from time to time to check that the license is still valid. When you first activate a license, KAM-Q sends your license key and the name of your computer, so the store can show which computers use the license. That name is whatever you or your IT team set it to, and it can include a person's name. Later checks send the license key and an installation ID. No mail, no mail addresses, and no index are ever sent. Licenses issued directly by us are checked on your computer and make no connection at all.
  • Web app. Your browser connects directly to Microsoft or Google. Sign-in tokens, settings, and the index are stored in your browser only. Signing out keeps the search index. Erase KAM-Q data removes the index for the connection currently in use; some browser preferences can remain. To remove all locally stored KAM-Q data for this web origin, clear its site data in your browser.
  • Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com in the web app. KAM-Q stores message details, attachment file names, and Microsoft's short message preview in your browser for local search. The preview can contain up to the first 255 characters of the message text. Optional deep search sends your query to Microsoft and can display additional search summaries. Those summaries are held temporarily for display and are not added to KAM-Q's persistent search index. When you choose an attachment, its contents are downloaded to your device. These mailbox requests go directly between your browser and Microsoft, not through a PRB mailbox processing server.
  • Gmail in the web app. KAM-Q reads message headers (sender, recipients, subject, date, labels), attachment file names, and a short preview of each message that Gmail provides, called a snippet. Snippets come from the message text. KAM-Q stores these in your browser so you can search them. It does not store full message bodies. When you open or save an attachment, KAM-Q asks Gmail for that message, and Gmail's response can include inline message content. The full message response is not written to KAM-Q's search index, and the attachment you choose is downloaded to your device. Deleting the index does not delete a file you have downloaded. Your browser connects directly to Google, and none of this goes to PRB Interactive Inc. or to any server we operate. If you turn on Cleanup, KAM-Q also asks for permission to move messages to Trash and to restore them. It does not permanently delete messages.
  • This website. It is a static site. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested. We also use Plausible Analytics to count visits to these pages. Plausible sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and collects no personal information. It records the page visited, the site that referred you, and a general country, device type, and browser. We use it only to see which pages people find useful. Plausible is not used in the KAM-Q web app, and it never receives anything about your mail.

Permissions KAM-Q asks for

  • Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com: User.Read to show who is signed in, and Mail.Read to read mail. Both are read-only.
  • Gmail: gmail.readonly to read your mail, asked for when you sign in. If you turn on the optional Cleanup feature, KAM-Q also asks for gmail.modify to move messages to Trash and to restore them. That one is first requested when you choose to move mail. Once you have turned Cleanup on, later sign-ins ask for it as well. It never deletes messages permanently.

How we use information

Information from your mailbox is used only to provide search and People features to you, inside your own browser or on your own computer. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared.

KAM-Q's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not share your mailbox data with anyone, because we do not receive it. Purchase and license details go to our store provider, as described below.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your index lives on your computer or in your browser. Delete the KAM-Q data folder, or choose Erase KAM-Q data in the web app, and the index is gone. You can also remove KAM-Q's access at any time in your Google account or your Microsoft account.

How we protect your data

KAM-Q is built so that your mail and search index are not sent to us, so there is no copy of them on our systems to lose. The specific measures below apply to the data KAM-Q does handle, including sensitive data such as the contents of your mailbox and the tokens that give access to it.

  • Encrypted connections. The connections KAM-Q makes to Google, to Microsoft, to the license service, and to this website use HTTPS.
  • Local storage and device protection. KAM-Q does not add application level encryption to its desktop or browser search index. Desktop index files are stored in the user's profile and are subject to the device's file permissions. The web index is stored in IndexedDB for the app's web origin. Protection of data on disk depends on the browser, operating system and device settings, including full disk encryption where enabled. Use a secured device and turn on FileVault or BitLocker where available.
  • Access control. Your data stays in your own user account on your own device. In the architecture described here, PRB Interactive Inc. runs no routine mailbox backend and has no administrative access to your mailbox, your index, or your tokens, because none of it is sent to a system we operate. This does not protect against a compromised device, browser or token, so keep them secure.
  • Authentication and tokens. Sign-in happens on Google's and Microsoft's own pages, never on ours. KAM-Q never sees, asks for, or stores your password. For Microsoft, KAM-Q uses the Microsoft Authentication Library and the OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow with PKCE. For Google, it uses Google Identity Services, which returns a short lived access token to your browser. In both cases the token is held only in your browser (the Microsoft Authentication Library keeps Microsoft sign-in state in local storage, and the Google token is kept in session storage), KAM-Q holds no client secret, and the provider only returns a token to a web address we have registered with them in advance. Signing out asks Google to revoke the token, asks Microsoft to end the sign-in session, and clears KAM-Q's saved sign-in state in your browser. It keeps your search index unless you choose to erase it.
  • Least privilege. KAM-Q requests the narrowest permissions that allow the product to work, and requests them incrementally. The optional Cleanup permission is first asked for when you choose to move mail, not at your first sign-in. The permissions in use are listed in full above.
  • Retention and deletion. We keep no copy of your mailbox data or search index, so there is nothing of that kind for us to retain. Records unrelated to your mailbox, such as purchases, licensing, support email and website logs, are held separately. On your side the index persists until you remove it. In the web app, Erase KAM-Q data removes the index for the connection you are using and clears the selected connection setting; some other preferences and data for another connection can remain. You can clear all site data for KAM-Q in your browser to remove anything else. On Windows and Mac, deleting the KAM-Q data folder removes the index. A file you downloaded or opened from an email stays on your device until you delete it. You can revoke KAM-Q's access at any time from your Google or Microsoft account, which invalidates its tokens without needing anything from us.
  • Code integrity. The Windows and Mac applications are code signed by PRB Interactive Inc., and the Mac applications are notarized by Apple, so you can confirm that what you installed is what we published and that it has not been altered.
  • Third parties. The only third parties involved are the identity and mail providers you choose to connect, our payment and licensing provider, which receives your license key, the name of your computer at activation, an installation identifier, and your purchase details, and never receives mail data, and the hosting and analytics providers for this website. KAM-Q does not send mailbox contents to our licensing provider, to the website's hosting or analytics providers, or to any server we operate. Google and Microsoft process your mail because you connect it to them.
  • Incident response. If we identify a security incident affecting customer data, we will assess its impact and notify affected users without undue delay using available contact details and, where appropriate, an update on this website. We will explain what is known, recommended actions and how to obtain further information, and provide updates as the investigation proceeds. To report a suspected vulnerability, write to support@kam-q.com and we will acknowledge it within two business days.

Because the data lives with you, the protections on your own devices matter. Keep your computer and browser up to date, use a password or passcode on your account, and turn on full disk encryption.
