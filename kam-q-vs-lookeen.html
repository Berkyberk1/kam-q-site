Where Lookeen is better

Lookeen searches documents and file shares as well as mail, and it has a server edition that indexes centrally for a whole company. If you are buying for an IT department that wants one index serving many people, Lookeen is built for that and KAM-Q is not. Lookeen has also been on the market since 2007.

Where KAM-Q is different

Mac and browser. KAM-Q runs on Windows and Mac, and has a browser version for Microsoft 365, Outlook.com and Gmail, all on one license.

Price. Lookeen's entry plan works out to €108 a year per person including VAT. KAM-Q renews at $19.99 a year and one license covers four installations. The two are priced differently, so compare what you actually need.

The People view. A ranked list of everyone who writes to you, when they write, and every file they have sent, with a search inside that file list for when it runs long.

You keep what you paid for if you stop renewing. KAM-Q keeps searching everything already indexed. On Windows and Mac your mail is indexed on your own computer.

Which to choose

Lookeen for a company that wants central indexing across documents and file shares. KAM-Q for a person who wants their own mail searchable instantly, on whichever computers they use, without an annual bill that makes them think twice.
