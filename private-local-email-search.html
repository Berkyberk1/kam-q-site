Where your mail and index live

  • Windows and Mac. KAM-Q reads your mail through Outlook or Apple Mail on your own computer and keeps its index in your own user folder.
  • Browser. The index is kept in your browser. The browser version talks straight from your browser to Microsoft or Google.
  • No KAM-Q server receives your mail.

What it does connect to

The apps make network connections for license checks and update checks. They do not send your mail or your searches. The privacy policy sets out exactly what goes where.

Other boundaries

  • The Outlook versions only read. They never send, move or delete a message.
  • Sign-in happens on Microsoft’s and Google’s own pages. KAM-Q does not see your password.
  • Gmail Cleanup is optional and off unless you turn it on. It moves a sender’s old mail to Trash only when you ask, with an Undo button.
  • The Windows installer is signed by PRB Interactive Inc. The Mac apps are signed and notarized by Apple.
