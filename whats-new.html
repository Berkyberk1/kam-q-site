What's new

Every KAM-Q release, and what changed in it.

Windows, for classic Outlook

Version 2.0.3 4 November 2026

  • KAM-Q no longer sends the name of your computer when you activate a licence. It sends a random identifier instead, made on your own machine, which tells your installations apart without saying anything about you or your computer. Nothing you have already activated is affected.
  • There is now a second installer for IT departments, which installs KAM-Q for every user on a machine rather than one. Most people want the ordinary one. If your company deploys software centrally, ask them about it.
  • That installer also carries a tidy-up script, so IT can clear KAM-Q's index from a computer when the program is removed. The ordinary installer still leaves your index alone, because it is yours.
  • No change to how search, People or attachments work.

Version 2.0.2 28 October 2026

  • Search now understands or, and, not, quoted phrases and brackets. Type invoice or contract to find either one, "purchase order" for that exact phrase, or put a minus in front of a word to leave it out. NOT needs capitals, because not is an ordinary word people search for.
  • The People view has a box for searching one person's files. It matches the file name and the subject of the email the file arrived with, so you can find something by remembering either.
  • A person's file list used to stop at the newest 500 files. It now shows all of them.
  • Minimizing KAM-Q tucks it into the notification area, by the clock, instead of leaving it in the taskbar. Click that icon to bring it back, or press Ctrl+Alt+Q from anywhere, including from inside Outlook. Right-click the icon to turn this off.
  • Corrected two things in the setup guide: one license covers four installations, not two, and renewing pays for keeping your index up to date with new mail as well as for new versions.

Version 2.0.1 13 October 2026

  • Your license now allows 30 days offline before KAM-Q needs to check it.
  • If a subscription ends, KAM-Q keeps working and everything already indexed stays searchable. New mail is no longer added to the index until you renew.
  • KAM-Q now shows its own icon in the taskbar.
  • Corrected the setup guide's description of when KAM-Q connects to the internet.

Version 2.0 12 October 2026

  • First public release.
  • Installer that needs no administrator rights.
  • Seven day trial, and licenses from our store.
  • Search, People, and attachments by sender, with the index kept on your own computer.

Mac, for classic Outlook

Version 3.4 4 November 2026

  • KAM-Q no longer sends the name of your computer when you activate a licence. It sends a random identifier instead, made on your own machine, which tells your installations apart without saying anything about you or your computer. Nothing you have already activated is affected.
  • The helper that builds the People view now travels inside the app itself, where it is covered by Apple's signature, instead of being written out beside your index. Nothing about using KAM-Q changes.
  • No change to how search, People or attachments work.

Version 3.2 13 October 2026

  • First public release for Mac.
  • Signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.
  • Seven day trial, and licenses from our store.
  • Your license now allows 30 days offline before KAM-Q needs to check it.
  • If a subscription ends, KAM-Q keeps working and everything already indexed stays searchable. New mail is no longer added to the index until you renew.
  • The window no longer offers a minimize button, which could not be undone on this kind of app.
