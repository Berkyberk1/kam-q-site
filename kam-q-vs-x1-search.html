Where X1 is better

X1 searches your whole computer, not just your mail. If you want one search box for documents, email and cloud storage together, X1 does that and KAM-Q does not. Its preview pane is excellent, you can reply and forward from the results, and its SharePoint and OneDrive connectors pull cloud documents into the same list. For a team already inside Microsoft 365, that matters.

Where KAM-Q is better

Price. Two years of KAM-Q cost $59.98 at the regular price, less than one year of X1 at $79.

Mac. X1 is Windows only. KAM-Q has a signed, notarized Mac app for classic Outlook and a separate one for Apple Mail, and one licence covers both.

A distinctive People view. KAM-Q ranks everyone who writes to you, shows when they tend to write, and gathers every file they have ever sent in one place.

You keep what you paid for. Stop renewing and KAM-Q still opens and still searches everything it has already indexed. Renewing pays for keeping the index current and for new versions, not for permission to use it.

Nothing leaves your computer. KAM-Q reads your mail through Outlook or Apple Mail and keeps its index in your own folder.

Which to choose

If you need to search documents and cloud storage alongside your mail, and the price is not an obstacle, X1 is the more complete tool. If what you actually lose time to is finding email, attachments and the people who sent them, and you want it on a Mac or at a fraction of the cost, that is what KAM-Q was built for.
