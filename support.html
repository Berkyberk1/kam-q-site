Where KAM-Q keeps its data

  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\KAM-Q
  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/KAM-Q (the Apple Mail version uses KAM-Q Apple Mail)
  • Web: inside your browser. Use Settings, then sign out and erase KAM-Q data from this browser.

Remove KAM-Q completely

Delete the KAM-Q program, the Desktop icon, and the data folder above. Your email is not touched, because KAM-Q only keeps its own search index.

Common questions

KAM-Q says it cannot connect to Outlook

Open Outlook first. If Outlook has a New Outlook switch at the top right, turn it off. KAM-Q works with classic Outlook.

Windows warns about the publisher, or says "Windows protected your PC"

KAM-Q is signed by PRB Interactive Inc. Right-click the file, choose Properties, then Digital Signatures to see it. The warning appears because the signing certificate is new and Defender SmartScreen has not built up a download history for it. Click More info, then Run anyway. On a managed work computer, ask your IT team to allow the PRB Interactive Inc. publisher.

What changed in the latest version?

Every release and what changed in it is listed on the What's new page.

What happens if I stop paying?

KAM-Q keeps working and everything it has already indexed stays searchable, for as long as you like. Nothing is deleted. What stops is reading new mail into the index, so Rescan and changing folders are unavailable until you renew. Renew and KAM-Q picks up where it left off.

KAM-Q says it needs to check my license

If you bought from our store, KAM-Q confirms the license from time to time. It can go 30 days without reaching us, so being offline or travelling is fine. After that it asks for a short internet connection to check. Searching is never affected.

Windows asks about an untrusted publisher, or says scripts are disabled

Your computer only allows approved programs. KAM-Q is signed by PRB Interactive Inc. Ask your IT team to allow that publisher.

The same email shows twice

A folder that holds copies of your mail is probably included. Open the folder list and untick the extra folder.

Indexing seems slow

Very large mailboxes take time, and KAM-Q reads newest mail first. You can keep using it while it works. Choosing fewer folders makes it much faster.
