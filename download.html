Check what you downloaded

KAM-Q is distributed only from this site and from our store. A copy from anywhere else may have been modified. Please take thirty seconds to check what you have.

On Windows: right-click the installer, choose Properties, then Digital Signatures. The publisher must read PRB Interactive Inc. If it says anything else, or there is no signature at all, do not run it.

On a Mac: our apps are signed and notarized by Apple, which means Apple has checked them for malware. A genuine KAM-Q opens without macOS warning you that the developer cannot be verified. If you see that warning, the copy is not ours.
