    How it works

    Three steps, and most of it happens in the background.

    1. Download and connectInstall the app and pick the folders you want to search. Your Inbox and its subfolders are chosen for you. In the browser there is nothing to install: you sign in and go.
    2. Let it indexKAM-Q reads your newest mail first, in batches, so you can search within minutes while older mail keeps loading.
    3. Search and exploreFind messages, open the People view, and pull up the files a person has sent, searching that list by name or subject.

    Your inbox is private. Your search should be too.

    This is the part worth reading properly, because it is the thing most search tools get wrong.

    • The index stays on your device. On Windows and Mac, KAM-Q reads your mail through Outlook or Apple Mail on your own computer and keeps its index in your own user folder. In the browser it is kept in your browser.
    • No KAM-Q server receives your mail. The browser version talks straight from your browser to Microsoft or Google. Your mail does not pass through us.
    • Read-only for Outlook. The Outlook versions only read. They never send, move or delete a message. That is a fixed boundary, not a setting.
    • Sign-in happens on Microsoft's and Google's own pages. KAM-Q never sees your password.
    • Gmail Cleanup is optional and careful. It moves a sender's old mail to Trash, only when you ask, with an Undo button. It is off unless you turn it on.
    • Signed software. The Windows installer is signed by PRB Interactive Inc. The Mac apps are signed and notarized by Apple.

    To be precise about what this does not claim: the apps do make network connections, for license checks and update checks. They do not send your mail or your searches. The privacy policy sets out exactly what goes where.

    Questions

    Does KAM-Q send my mail anywhere?

    No. The Windows and Mac versions keep their index on your own computer. The browser version connects your browser straight to Microsoft or Google. We run no server that sees your mail. Full detail in the privacy policy.

    Which Outlook does it work with?

    The desktop apps need classic Outlook, on Windows or Mac. The new Outlook is not supported. The browser version works with any Microsoft 365, Outlook.com or Gmail account, whichever Outlook you use.

    Does it change or delete my email?

    The Outlook versions only read. In Gmail, the optional Cleanup feature moves a sender's old mail to Trash when you ask, with an Undo button. KAM-Q never deletes anything permanently.

    I have a very large mailbox. Is that a problem?

    No. KAM-Q reads mail newest first and in batches, so you can search within minutes while it works. A 400,000 message mailbox is a normal size for it. You can also choose which folders to include.

    What happens if I cancel?

    Everything already indexed stays searchable, for as long as you like. New mail stops being added to the index, and you stop getting new versions. Refunds within 30 days, any reason.
